# On The Route: your first day (one page)

For trainees. Your trainer has the full guide; this is what you need to start.

**Signing in**
- Type your employee ID and the password your trainer gives you. The first time, you choose your own password.
- Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes; your trainer can unlock it at once.
- **Settings → Accessibility**: language, larger text, colours, keys and narration (the game reads the screen aloud).

**What the stars mean**

Every module and every workday gives you 0 to 3 stars in **safety**, **efficiency** and **service**. A **critical**
mistake (for example driving through a red light, into a no-entry street, past a school bus with its lights flashing, or
handing an age-restricted parcel over without checking ID) holds that category to one star, however well the rest goes.
Mistakes are how you learn: the **Debrief** after each day shows what went well and what to fix, and the **drive map**
puts a pin where each driving mistake happened.

**In the 3D workday**

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **W A S D** or the arrows | walk, or drive and steer in the cab |
| **E** | use what you are looking at (a door, a parcel, a control in the cab) |
| **P** | step through what you can use nearby, from the keyboard |
| **H** | the menu: controls and guidance, settings, the workday steps |
| **Tab** | the handheld (scanner, route, workday details) |
| **B** / **Space** | seatbelt / parking brake |
| **Q** / **C** | indicate left / right |
| **R** | reverse: the rear camera comes on by itself |
| **L** / **K** | headlights / wipers |
| **J** | read aloud the road sign you are looking at |

**Good habits the game looks for**
- Look at the left mirror before moving off, and the mirror on the side you turn to before you steer.
- Follow the sat nav on the dash. Stop fully at stop signs, give way where the sign says, keep to the speed limit.
- Stop at a red light. Look for cyclists on the right and children at the school crossing.
- Secure the load before you drive, and drive gently with it. Your van is 10 feet 10 inches tall: avoid low bridges.
- At every door: the right parcel, scanned, and the checks the parcel needs (signature, photo ID).

**Stopping half-way**
The workday saves as you go. To stop, open the menu (**H**) and save and return to the hub. Come back on **the same
computer** to carry on where you were: on another computer the day starts again from the morning (the game asks first).

**Need help?** Press **H** in the 3D workday, or ask your trainer.
