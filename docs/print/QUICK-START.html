Trainer's quick start (one page)

For the person running a session. Print this page (on one A4 sheet: docs/print/QUICK-START.html); the full guide is docs/PILOT-GUIDE.md. The game's other 11 languages each have this page as docs/QUICK-START.<lang>.md (machine translations, awaiting native review), and its A4 sheet in docs/print/.

Before trainees arrive

  1. Open the game on one training PC and start a workday. If it says "WebGL is required", ask IT to turn graphics acceleration on (chrome://gpu should say "WebGL: Hardware accelerated").
  2. Settings → Trainer (your PIN): create accounts if trainees sign in with an employee ID (New account; the temporary password is shown once).
  3. Same screen: Academy rules (pass mark, attempts) and 3D workday aids… (driving assists, sat nav, night runs, van damage). "Fade by day" gives full help on day 1, less on days 2–3, little from day 4. Press Save rules.

The session (about an hour)

TimeWhat
10 minWelcome. The point is safe habits, not speed; mistakes are the lesson.
5 minSign in. Settings → Accessibility: language, larger text, colours, keys, narration.
30 minEach trainee plays a workday. It saves as they go; the pause menu lets them stop and resume later on the same PC (on another PC the day starts again; the game asks first).
15 minDebrief together: Results → Debrief, then the drive map and Replay the last mistake. (the seconds before it, from the driver's seat). Ask: what was the hardest stop, and what would you do differently?

Show them the cab (one minute)

  • Look at a mirror to check it; the left mirror before moving off, the turning side before steering.
  • Signal: Q left, C right, for at least three seconds before moving off (backwards too) or turning, then the mirror.
  • Gears: look at the selector and press E, or R for reverse; the rear camera comes on by itself.
  • Sat nav on the dash: the route, the next turn and how far. A missed turn reroutes; rejoin within 30 seconds.
  • Road signs: stop signs, give way, speed limits, school zones, one-way, no entry, road works, traffic lights. J reads the sign in view aloud.
  • H opens the controls and guidance at any time; P steps through the cab controls from the keyboard.

From the fourth workday (tell them before they start)

  • Walk round the van before the first drive and again at the depot after the last stop: P to an area, E, then the description that matches. A scrape they caused and do not record is a critical mistake.
  • The dispatch radio calls once on the first drive: stop, set the parking brake, then read it on the handheld.
  • Night every third day from day 6: headlights (L) on before moving.

Reading the drive map: a pin at every driving mistake. A diamond is a road sign or red light, a star is damage to the van, a triangle a low bridge, a hexagon a shifted load, an arrow a wrong turn, a plus a cab habit (signal, mirror, belt or lights), a circle anything else. Critical mistakes (a red light, no entry, a school bus) cap that category at one star.

When something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
Slow or jerky 3DSettings → Graphics → Low; close other tabs; plug the laptop in.
Cannot sign inCheck the ID; five wrong tries lock it 15 minutes; Manage → New password unlocks it.
Progress seems lostSame employee ID? The server keeps progress per ID.
Text looks wrong in a languageNote the screen and the words for the native reviewers (docs/review/).
The server is downTrainees keep playing; progress is sent when it is back. Ask IT to restart it.
