# Visible people and street detail

C-51 is started on `codex/c51-visible-people`, stacked on the pushed C-50 renderer. The uploaded source adapter and bounded renderer are implemented; current-pilot acceptance is in progress.

Current residents in fpactors.js appear only after contact, and only the nearest unresolved resident is visible. The courier is a reflection actor, and the school crossing is a separate safety encounter. Preserve those behaviors while adding bounded ambient people that are visible during ordinary routes.

The owner’s Mini Characters archive contains 12 animated adults with 32 clips each, including idle/walk/sit, two skins and an external palette image supplied inside the archive. Their lowercase clip names need an explicit adapter to the existing actor presentation. The Furniture Kit bench has 170 triangles and no texture. Raw model, animation, joint-count, image, licence and SHA-256 evidence is in `review/set-10/c51-model-audit/`. Original archive licences are CC0. The initial audit shipped no files; the two prepared packs are now integrated below.

Plan at most two High ambient adults and one Low person in bounded, visible front-yard strips on non-delivery lots. Keep body/path bounds clear of road lanes, public walking corridors, houses, props and every delivery approach; use actual rendered bounds. Short idle/walk loops stay in those strips. Reduced motion holds idle. No new control, report, save field or collision rule is needed. High must stay at four textures by baking palette colours to vertices; keep the old procedural actor fallback for missing packs. Reserve the Low person’s measured triangle cost from existing town decoration before selection, rather than adding it on top: the earlier CPU sweep peaked at 2,492/2,500.

The renderer is now connected. High loads two prepared Mini Characters adults and up to four safely placed Furniture Kit benches. The source adult mesh is fitted to 1.7 m with narrower horizontal proportions, with a 0.6 m swept body radius verified through walk samples and turns. Low requests no new pack and uses a 36-triangle, three-part silhouette in the existing route batch. Its cost is reserved from town decoration after selecting collision-bearing parked cars. The tight combined budget stays 60; optional Low people are hidden when fewer than 36 triangles remain after those cars. Existing obstruction visibility takes priority. High and missing-model fallback use the same safe placements. The bench keeps its original wood material factor; it has no vertex-colour attribute, so its shared town shader explicitly disables that attribute. No new control, text, event type, saved field, collider, texture or reflection pass is added.

The source conversion retains the adults' exact positions, normals, triangles, joint weights, inverse bind matrices and idle/walk samples. It bakes the supplied archive palette into linear RGB vertices and removes UVs/images and the other 30 clips. `test/tools/check-ambient-source.py` verifies those facts against both original archives and reproduces all three outputs byte for byte. Source hashes and original CC0 licences accompany the two packs. Credits were regenerated with the tool Claude authorised.

The source adapter and renderer remain in progress until the current-pilot Node run, native people/fallback/accessibility views and combined fpbench resource gate are complete. Early Low sweeps cover 285,600 views each: loaded 60 calls / 2,472 triangles, fallback 59 calls / 2,196 triangles before the priority correction; final corrected sweeps pending. These sweeps include all existing controls, mirrors/rear camera, cargo, traffic, navigation, richer stops, damage and walkaround. They are CPU geometry evidence; cloud frame rates do not certify Intel 60 fps (O-1).

Nine world integration tests now include the combined delivery/damage/navigation case: with loaded and missing models, enabling ambient detail selects exactly the same collision-bearing cars as the original town selector. All 60 route-coverage cases have people near at least one planned driving leg (Low 180/180 legs, High 178/180 within selection range). Coverage is potential range, not pixel proof for every route. Native pixel views and the full resource gate are separate.
