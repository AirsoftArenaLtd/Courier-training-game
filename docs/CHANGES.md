# What changed

For trainers and the training lead: what a trainer or trainee will notice, newest first. The details for IT are in
`docs/IT-NOTES.md`, and the day-to-day record of the work is the log in `docs/COORDINATION.md`. A milestone reaches
the training PCs when the owner merges `pilot` into `main`.

## Milestone 8 so far: a town that looks real, with traffic (to 20 October 2026)

**In the 3D workday**
- The town, the van and the people are 3D models now, under real lighting: morning, midday, dusk or night, with the
  headlights to switch on at dusk and at night. Night runs can be turned off in **3D workday aids…**.
- Weather you can see: rain on the windscreen (the wipers clear it), fog, snow and ice.
- Road signs where they would be in a town: street names and house numbers, stop and give-way signs, speed limits,
  school zones, one-way and no-entry streets and road works. **J** reads the sign in view aloud.
- A sat nav on the dashboard with the route, the next turn and how far. A missed turn reroutes; the trainee has 30
  seconds to rejoin before it counts as leaving the route.
- From the third workday, traffic that behaves: traffic lights, a give-way junction, a cyclist passing on the right
  and children at the school crossing. Driving through a red light or into a no-entry street is a critical mistake.
- Less writing on the screen: hints when they are needed, nothing covering the road.

**After the drive**
- The drive map shows a shape for each kind of mistake: a diamond for a road sign or traffic light, an arrow for a
  wrong turn, a star for damage to the van, a triangle for a low bridge, a hexagon for a shifted load.
- The debrief, the record and the drive map are checked in all 12 languages at both text sizes.

**For the trainer**
- **Before and after** now shows road habits (red lights, wrong turns, give way, cyclists) for trainees who have
  driven with traffic, and can be downloaded as a spreadsheet as well as printed.
- The printed record, the certificate and the report print correctly in the Indian scripts.
- A one-page quick start (`docs/QUICK-START.md`), also in Tamil and Hindi.
- Button labels such as "Debrief ▶" and "Next ▶", and warnings such as "⚠ Ran a red light", now appear in the
  trainee's language; they used to stay in English.

**For the company's LMS**
- Both SCORM packages (1.2 and 2004) carry everything the 3D workday needs, and a day in progress is saved more
  compactly, so even the busiest day keeps its drive map when a trainee resumes it.

## Milestone 7: a van that feels right (to 18 October 2026)

- The van drives like a van: steering, weight, braking and a load that changes how it handles. The camera leans into
  turns and eases with speed.
- A real cab: gauges, the P-R-N-D selector, handbrake, indicators, lights and wipers are controls in the cab, used
  by looking at them or with the keyboard (**P** steps through them and says what each does).
- Mirrors are always live. A trainee checks one by looking at it; the left mirror before moving off and the mirror
  on the turning side before steering are marked on the results.
- Selecting reverse switches on the rear camera with guidelines, distance marks and beeps. **Choose a reversing
  space** in the 3D menu offers three spaces to practise on.
- **Settings → Trainer → 3D workday aids…**: driving assists, the sat nav, the walk-round before a tight reverse,
  night runs and van damage. "Fade by day" gives full help on the first workday and less after that.
- Everything new is translated into all 11 languages and checked at larger text.
