## Controls to review

Walk with W/A/S/D and use Shift for careful steps. Arrow keys or the mouse look around. P faces a nearby visible target; E interacts. F inspects the carried package, Tab opens the handheld, and H opens guidance. The four controls pages display actual remaps. The native sequence exercises P→O, E→U and F→I rather than assuming defaults.

Targets still require an actual unobscured ray. Cargo focus uses its label face so the top parcel can be selected above the shelf lip. Doors, cart, parcels, empty positions and restraints are physical targets. Panels and handheld pages stop both clocks. Assessment hides coaching; abandoning an assessment uses an attempt without recording a completed play.
