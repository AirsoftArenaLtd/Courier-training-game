# B-134: delivery handheld Back clearance

Status: review on `codex/b134-handheld-layout`, from pilot `d1caa22e`. Product source `66440652`; the keyboard test timing correction is `a701a65e`.

The 3D handheld reserved a fixed 30-pixel footer strip unless it had a footer sentence. Back is its own text control, so taller font metrics could extend into the last choice. The owned `fpAccess.layoutDevice` adapter now measures Back and reserves an eight-pixel gap. Dense translated pages compact gaps between choices from six toward two pixels to preserve space for their body text. Text, full click targets, wording and keyboard routes retain their sizes and behavior.

Claude reported the overlap in Tamil with larger text. The unchanged full doorstep suite passes here (394 checks), so that original failure was not reproduced with this machine's font metrics. The new taller-metric geometry regression fails before the correction. Its first footer-only version crowded the larger Tamil delivery body; that intermediate failed capture is retained separately. A second regression protects both body and Back boundaries.
