# C-24: rear camera and parking aids

17 October 2026, `codex/c24-rear-camera`. Started stacked on C-23; moved onto pilot `153c3a8` after C-23 merged.
Selecting R automatically powers the physical dash screen. Steering guidelines, distance bands, sensor bars,
quiet beeps and whole-sentence distance cues support reversing without a mandatory get-out check.

The initial checkpoints, cargo and travel positions are fixtures. Menus, physical shifter selection, camera
selection, steering, acceleration, braking and the parking brake use real keys. Temporary coloured cubes prove
that the displayed image observes current world geometry; they are removed before the screenshots. A separate
real-key campaign test reports safe backing, then an actual collision with a building, including camera viewing
time and one resumed manoeuvre key. Overspeed and a brake applied while still rolling have model regressions.

Practice is isolated from workday scoring and from the suspended workday/cargo checkpoints. The three spaces
have physical walls and marked completion bays. The trainer-required walk-around is limited to the tight-drive
scenario; normal backing uses the automatic camera. The guide explains the shifter, keyboard screen selection
and when a walk-around is needed. All new text goes through Phaser Text. Claude translates after merge, so new
sentences use English fallback in the Tamil captures; existing Tamil interface text and Noto fonts remain.
The new panels use 19 px body text normally and 23 px with larger text, with no shrinking or overlap. Their
largest Phaser texture estimate is 2,598,203 bytes, within the same Low limit.

The low-resource ceilings are unchanged, and the rear camera shares C-23's atlas. The browser gates use the
software renderer; the native Intel 60 fps acceptance check remains O-1. Details and commands are in
`docs/3D-REAR-CAMERA.md` and `docs/PERF.md`. No shared save, scoring, Settings or translation source was edited.
