# C-23: physical live mirrors

17 October 2026, `codex/c23-live-mirrors`. Started stacked on C-22 `08efa85`; C-22 merged during this task.
The left, right and interior mirrors are physical parts of the van with live reflected geometry. Look at one
for 0.4 seconds to establish a check; the matching check must precede the manoeuvre by no more than four seconds.
There is no mirror button. Mouse, remapped arrows and keyboard target selection all reach the same gaze path.

The route/cargo/stationary location are fixtures. Looking, target selection and moving off use real keys.
Temporary red and blue 3D cubes in front of each reflection camera prove that each physical face changes when
the world changes; they are removed before the screenshots. The clip includes those pixel probes. The interior
mirror sees the actual cab/bulkhead; the automatic rear camera belongs to C-24. C-24 also replaces the old
get-out-and-look backing score, so this branch's backing instructions are still the previous version.

The guide and mirror hints use whole English sentences through Phaser Text. Claude translates after merge;
new sentences fall back to English in Tamil, while existing interface text uses its Tamil dictionary. The flow
asserts the selected language after scene restart, and uses installed Noto fonts. Larger text and reduced motion
are tested together; no pointer lock is needed. Lifecycle pauses preserve a saved partial check, while an
explicitly opened menu cancels partial dwell. Old button timestamps cannot establish evidence.

The fixed Low ceilings remain 64 calls, 2,500 triangles, two textures and 4 MiB each for attachments and the Phaser
texture estimate. Both 162,000-view sweeps and rendered samples pass. SwiftShader frame rates are not the native
Intel acceptance measurement; O-1 remains with the owner. Implementation: `docs/3D-MIRRORS.md`; commands and
figures: `docs/PERF.md`. Shared scoring, save, settings and translation sources are unchanged.
