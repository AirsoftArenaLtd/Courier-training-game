# Milestone 7 review: five minutes (owner)

Milestone 7 is "a van that feels right". Compare `docs/review/set-6/views/` (before) with `docs/review/set-7/views/` (after),
then play for five minutes with the steps below. Write one line per item: fine, or what feels wrong. That line goes to the
board as the first task of the next round.

**Before you start:** `git pull` the `pilot` branch, serve the folder, open `index.html`, start a workday.

| # | Try this | It should feel like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pull away, drive to the first junction, stop at the line (C-20, C-21) | A heavy van: it takes a moment to get going, leans a little into turns and dips when you brake, but it goes where you point it |
| 2 | Turn left and right at a junction at 10 mph | The back wheels follow the turn and the nose swings out; no sliding |
| 3 | Look at the left and right mirrors without pressing anything (C-23) | Real mirrors on the van, always showing what is behind |
| 4 | Look at the gear shifter and press the action key to choose R (C-22) | Like reaching for the lever; the dashboard shows the gear |
| 5 | Reverse into the space behind a parked car (C-24) | The rear camera comes on by itself, the lines bend with the steering, beeps speed up as you get close; no need to get out |
| 6 | Use the indicator stalk, the lights switch, the wipers, the handbrake and the belt in the cab (C-22) | Each one is a thing in the cab you look at and use; the keyboard keys still work |
| 7 | Play a second and a fourth workday (A-21) | Less help each day; a trainer can turn aids off in Settings → Trainer → 3D workday aids |
| 8 | Turn on larger text and reduced motion (Settings → Access) and drive again | Everything still readable; no camera sway with reduced motion |

Anything else you notice (looks, sound, confusion, frame rate on your laptop) goes at the bottom.
