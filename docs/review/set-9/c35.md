1. Compare the [rear-left wheel target](c35-surfaces/c35-final-high-en-en-normal-pre-wheel.png)
   and [roof-edge damage](c35-surfaces/c35-final-high-en-en-normal-post-roof.png).
   The crosshair selects the visible surface, with no climbing or camera teleport.
2. Inspect the [handheld condition record](c35-memory/c35-memory-after-ta-large-post-record.png)
   and [post-trip guidance](c35-memory/c35-memory-after-ta-large-guide-report.png).
   Text remains at readable sizes; new sentences still use English fallback.
3. In a new day 4+ with damage enabled, inspect and record each area before driving.
   Close cargo before the rear check. Existing wear must not create a damage penalty.
4. After a contact, return the van and scan retained parcels at Returns. With empty
   hands and cargo closed, inspect again and record the visible condition. Review
   the records through the handheld's workday details, then finish at Dispatch.
5. Repeat a checkpoint and finish: there must be no duplicate report. In a separate
   run, omit a caused zone to see the critical report, or describe a clear zone as
   damaged to see the once-only false-report penalty.
