# C-35 walkaround and damage report

Ready for review on `codex/c35-walkaround`, retaining pilot `74cd60a`.
New day-four-and-later workdays let the trainee inspect seven physical areas of the
parked van and record their visible condition on the handheld. Existing damage is
assessed at departure; player-caused damage is assessed at guarded finish. Missing
or incorrect post-trip reports use the existing critical check. False reports in
clear areas count once per zone. Records survive checkpoints without replaying
reports. First days, older checkpoints and trainer damage-off keep their behavior.
[Behavior and shared-QA recipe](../../3D-WALKAROUND.md).

The roof edge is reachable from the ground; the wheel follows the imported model.
Cargo remains accessible at both its door centre and original handle. Rear
inspection uses a separate part of the closed door. The guide now explains signs,
navigation, traffic, clearance, cargo and the actual pre/post controls. The handheld
uses remapped controls, narration and readable larger text. Claude's translation
pass is pending: 45 lines and four templates were identified in the early review.

   and [roof-edge damage](c35-cargo/c35-cargo-native-high-en-en-normal-post-roof.png).
   Both can be inspected from the ground.
3. Open the [larger-text handheld record](c35-cargo/c35-cargo-native-low-ta-ta-large-post-record.png)
   and [post-trip guidance](c35-cargo/c35-cargo-native-low-ta-ta-large-guide-report.png).
   New sentences still use English fallback pending translation.
4. In a new day 4+ with damage enabled, inspect each area with the remapped P/E
   controls and record its visible condition. Close cargo for rear inspection.
   Existing wear must not become a player-caused damage penalty.
5. After a contact, return and scan retained parcels, then inspect with empty hands
   and record the visible condition. Finish at Dispatch. In separate runs, omit a
   caused zone for the critical check or report wear in a clear area for the
   once-only false-report penalty; repeat a checkpoint to check no duplicate.
