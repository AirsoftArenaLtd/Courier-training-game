## Focused live checks

At an apartment/gated entry, select the physical intercom, call the matching entry,
identify the delivery and wait for the buzz. A locked gate remains solid; an unanswered
call leads to retention. At reception, record the named recipient and matching signature
before handover. At a locker, open its assigned compartment, scan the actual parcel,
deposit, close, photograph and record. Reopening invalidates the photo; retrieve the
parcel before an exception. A failed delivery needs the card, actual reason and next-day
afternoon reattempt, then the existing cargo/Returns scanner path. Save/resume at each
step preserves custody and never replays reports. Travel poses in the automated probes
are fixtures; controls, scanning, signature, reports and restores use the real paths.

sequentially:
