## Five-minute review

1. Start a fresh first workday. Its existing briefing, loading and simple doorstep path
   remain unchanged. On a later workday with a locker stop, read the briefing: exactly one eligible parcel
   is authorized for the named locker site; other signature and photo-ID duties remain.
2. At an apartment or gated site, select its physical intercom with the nearby-target
   key and use the action key. Call the matching entry, identify the delivery and wait
   outside for the buzz. A gate remains solid until unlocked. An unanswered call leads
   to retention rather than a fabricated unlock.
3. At reception, confirm the address and named recipient, capture the matching signature,
   then record the handover. At the locker, open its assigned compartment, scan the
   actual parcel, deposit it, close the door and confirm the photo. Reopening invalidates
   the photo; retrieving restores physical custody before an exception or cargo return.
4. For a failed delivery, leave the card, record its actual reason, book the offered
   next-day afternoon attempt and retain the parcel. Return it through the existing
   cargo/Returns scanner path. Saving and resuming during each protocol retains the steps
   and never replays scoring reports.
5. In the cab, look straight ahead at red/amber/green, give-way and stop approaches.
   The physical sign, selector positions and working gauges remain visible. Repeat with
   larger text and Tamil; the road sentences wrap above the dashboard at readable size.
