## Five-minute owner review

1. Start a new day three or later. Approach a physical signal; read it with J (or its
   remapped key) and narration. Stop before the line at red, then proceed at green. The
   drive review should show one successful red-light check and no duplicate stop check.
2. On a separate run, cross on red. Expect one critical red-light check. Watch a car stop
   and resume with the signal, and leave space for cross traffic at the give-way.
3. Approach the school crossing slowly. Watch both children wait, cross after a gap and
   stay solid at the curbs. Wait until they are clear. Try walking toward them too.
4. On day four, move slowly and look at the real right mirror when the cyclist passes.
   Wait for the bicycle to clear before turning right. A cut-in should fail once; driving
   straight should not manufacture a cyclist grade. A cyclist stopped at red must remain.
5. Resume a checkpoint while waiting, then select reverse: signals/actors should restore
   without a new report and the automatic rear camera should take over the sat nav. Check
   Low with larger text, colour-blind mode and reduced motion on the Intel laptop; record
   native FPS for O-1. Compare High and the missing-model fallback if time permits.
