# C-29: the integrated quality gate

18 October 2026. Checked source: `9958c65be84897fa0d972996dae12a2c3271ab8a`.

C-29 adds a repeatable review gate for the integrated cab, mirrors, rear camera, lighting,
weather, local town, van/people and compact display. High mirror housings now sit behind their
reflective faces. Targets, gaze, scoring, saves, controls, translations and Settings retain their
behavior. This is stacked on pushed C-47 `55acbe3`, including C-28 and B-30 over pilot `951ef36`;
Claude merges the stack in order.

Low additionally enables larger text, colour filtering and reduced motion.

## Mirror regression caught by this gate

High's 25 mm mirror housings previously extended 4.5 mm in front of their reflective faces
(before image at `b480a5d`; the geometry regression was added before applying the fix).
Moving their centers 17 mm back puts the full thickness behind the faces, without adding
geometry, materials, textures or passes; Low geometry stays identical.

[failed geometry test before the fix](c29/mirror-depth-before.log), and
[after](c29/c29-loaded-high-high-mirrorInterior.png) retain the defect and correction.
All eight sampled mirror views must now reflect a red marker changing to blue; temporary
markers are removed and disposed before measured frames and screenshots.

## Five-minute review

1. Compare the loaded Low/High cab, mirrors, resident and cargo images; source detail may differ,
   but physical controls, targets and paths must remain legible.
2. Compare the matching missing-model cab, reverse, cargo and resident views. Missing packs must
   leave a usable procedural world with the same controls and automatic rear camera.
3. Inspect larger-text scanning, storm and reverse for overlap, readable captions and retained
   warnings. Use C-47's Tamil evidence for translated layout coverage; new C-47 text awaits Claude.
4. Open the summary and raw reports: verify every named view, resource peak, actual quality,
   renderer identity, model fixture and no errors/context loss.
5. Reproduce `node test/fpquality.js` as described in [the gate guide](../../3D-QUALITY-GATE.md).
   For native Intel acceptance, use the documented O-1 command on the owner's laptop and retain
   average/worst-1% results plus a real interactive drive.

Cloud ANGLE SwiftShader resource figures do not establish Intel 60 fps. C-28's unchanged CPU
sweeps still cover 264,000 loaded and 264,000 fallback views (63/2,480 and 56/2,058 peaks).
