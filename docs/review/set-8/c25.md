# C-25: lighting, sky and time of day

17 October 2026, `codex/c25-lighting`, based on pilot `419e361`. The route follows its existing saved
clock, with four optional saved presets. Physical/keyboard headlights illuminate world surfaces, the
reverse lamp is automatic and night instruments remain readable. Low uses a texture-free sky and lamp
lighting; High adds bounded soft shadows, contact shading and stars. Reduced motion removes glare.

Travel, clock presets and viewpoints are fixtures. The headlight test presses the real L shortcut and
reads road pixels with the lamps off and on. High's comparison captures independently disable shadows
and ambient contact shading and require visible pixel differences. Shader/page errors and the GL
error state are checked. No temporary comparison setting is saved into the campaign.

The language audit refreshes translated text and uses the game's actual 17 px caption size, 18 px
with larger text. Existing Tamil text is translated; new lighting sentences use English fallback
until Claude's catalogue pass. Caption bounds, exact size, fixed reduced-motion FOV and disabled glare
are asserted in English and Tamil at both sizes.

The integration checkout applies the C-25 code to pushed C-24 `2472760` without merging or modifying
either review branch. Both adapters share the existing reflection atlas. The Low ceilings remain
64 calls, 2,500 triangles, two Three.js textures, 4 MiB attachments and 4 MiB estimated Phaser textures.
The cloud browser uses SwiftShader; O-1 remains the native Intel performance gate. Commands and figures
are in `docs/PERF.md`; implementation details are in `docs/3D-LIGHTING.md`.

No shared save, scoring, Settings or i18n source was edited. The first day's schedule and workday day
number stay unchanged. New text is whole English sentences; C-36 adds night scenarios and grading.
