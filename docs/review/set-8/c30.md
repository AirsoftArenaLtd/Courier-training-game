## Five-minute review

1. Compare normal/larger Low, High and larger Tamil. Sentences must remain readable, in the viewport and clear
   of captions, navigation, physical mirrors and the rear screen.
2. Watch the clip. The remapped reading key reads a physical sign without opening a device; Controls immediately
   hides the projected sign labels.
3. Inspect names/numbers and every regulatory shape. The depot give-way faces departure; roadworks and one-way
   posts have separate positions; the arrow points east and no-entry faces the prohibited approach.
4. On day three, both marked lanes may travel east. Prohibited entry reports no-entry once, and a failed signed
   stop reports sign-stop once without a legacy duplicate. An older checkpoint keeps its original types/rules.
5. Compare the gate/sweep reports with the ceilings. Reproduce `node test/fpquality.js` as in the gate guide;
   use the owner’s Intel laptop for O-1’s native frame-rate measurement.
