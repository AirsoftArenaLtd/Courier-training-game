# Milestone 8 review (so far): five minutes (owner)

Milestone 8 is "a town that looks real". Compare `docs/review/set-7/views/` (before) with `docs/review/set-8/views/` (after;
open `index.md` there: the same views at Low and High, plus a new view at the first door; `compare.md` puts set 7 and set 8 side by side). The sat nav and the walk-round
damage report are not built yet; they come later in the milestone. Then play for five minutes with the steps below. Write
one line per item: fine, or what feels wrong. That line goes to the board as the first task of the next round.

**Before you start:** `git pull` the `pilot` branch, serve the folder, open `index.html`, start a workday.

| # | Try this | It should feel like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Walk round the depot, then drive the first street | Houses, shops, trees, street furniture and parked cars made from real 3D models, not boxes; the town still reads clearly |
| 2 | Look at the van from outside, then the resident at the first door and the other courier in the depot | A proper van; people who look like people (they do not change your score) |
| 3 | Drive for a minute and notice what is on the screen | Less writing on the screen: hints when you need them, the dash card for where you are going, nothing covering the road |
| 4 | In the cab, look at a control and listen (narration on in Settings → Access) | The cab reads out what you are looking at and what it does, in your language |
| 5 | On the title screen, press Credits | The 3D packs and who made them, with thanks |
| 6 | Turn on Low graphics (Settings) and drive the same street | Plainer, but the same town and nothing missing that you need to drive |
| 7 | Turn on larger text, then switch to Tamil or Hindi and look at the cab and the guide (the same fixed views in Tamil with larger text are in `views-ta-large/`) | Everything still fits and is readable; say if the lower part of the cab view feels too full of words |
| 8 | Drive to the first stop following the sat nav, and stop at the first junction (views `satnav` and `signs`) | The dash screen tells you the next turn and how far; the stop sign and street names are where you expect. Say if the screen hides a sign you need to see (in `signs-high.jpg` it covers part of the stop sign on the right) |

Anything else you notice (looks, sound, confusion, frame rate on your laptop) goes at the bottom.
