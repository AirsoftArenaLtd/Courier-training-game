# C-28: the van, courier and residents

18 October 2026. `codex/c28-van-characters` is stacked on pushed B-30 `5667be9`, over pilot
`951ef36`. C-27 is merged. Claude merges the narration fix before this branch.

The delivery van now uses the owner's Kenney model, with company body/livery colours, steering
wheels and a lifting cargo door. The courier is visible in live reflections on foot. Residents
walk up, wave and talk at their original facade targets, and the door opens inward. Their shirt
and skin colours match the existing ID checks. Resumed conversations continue without replaying
the arrival. Missing or invalid actor files retain the procedural van and people.

Low uses a 190-triangle van and 88-triangle figures; High keeps source geometry. Ordinary meshes
are CPU-posed at 10/20 Hz without a bone texture. At most one nearby resident uses the rig; the
crossing figure and reflected courier also have distance bounds. Seven scene-local figure buffers
are the fixed maximum. Reduced motion uses a still pose and immediate doors. The lifting door
stays inside the original van footprint throughout its motion.

Every original collision, inspection, cab, cargo, shelf, parcel and doorstep target remains.
Scores, saves and shared Settings/i18n are unchanged. The interior mirror uses the retained cab
shell so the source windshield cannot obstruct its unchanged camera; other reflections use the
new exterior. Existing keyboards, actual mirror gaze and automatic rear camera remain available.
Resumed absent or resolved contacts keep closed doors, and legacy checkpoints retain their original
parcel visibility range.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, serve the site and set `QA_BASE` to its URL for browser suites.
The CPU sweeps are:

```sh
QA_ASSETS=1 QA_LATER=1 QA_CAMERA=1 QA_CAB=1 QA_MIRRORS=1 QA_REAR=1 QA_LIGHTING=1 QA_WEATHER=1 QA_CARGO=1 QA_TAG=c28-loaded node test/fproute.js
QA_LATER=1 QA_CAMERA=1 QA_CAB=1 QA_MIRRORS=1 QA_REAR=1 QA_LIGHTING=1 QA_WEATHER=1 QA_CARGO=1 QA_TAG=c28-fallback node test/fproute.js
node test/tools/validate-actors.js assets/3d
node test/fpactors.js
python3 test/fpactorsprepare.py
```

`QA_FILM=1 QA_GFX=low node test/fpactorsbrowser.js` captures 24 deterministic simulation poses
at 0.1-second intervals. The review clip encodes those captured frames at 10 fps (2.4 seconds);
it is an animation demonstration, not a frame-rate measurement. Its cargo opening and resident
contact are real E/Enter actions; travel, elapsed time and weather are named fixtures.

The runtime/conversion notes are in `docs/3D-ACTORS.md`; final figures are in `docs/PERF.md`.
The initial `c28-prepared-validation.json` is historical source inspection, not a runtime gate.
