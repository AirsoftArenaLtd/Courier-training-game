# Open questions for the translation reviewers

Made by `node test/tools/open-questions.js` from `docs/review/questions.json`; do not edit by hand. 25 questions in 9 languages.

Each question is also in the Comment column of that language's sheet (`docs/review/<language>-safety.csv`), on its line,
and those lines come first. Send this page with the sheet, so the reviewer knows what we most want to hear about.

## Bengali (bn-safety.csv)

1. **The line:** "Contacted the closure instead of following the diversion."
   The English says the van hit the closure (a contact). Does this say it hit it, or only that it reached it? If only reached, please correct.

2. **The line:** "Turned or changed lanes without a current check of the matching mirror."
   The English asks for a current (recent) mirror check. Does the translation keep "current"?

3. **The line:** "Never leave a signature-required package unattended."
   Most lines say সই, but this one and about 20 others say স্বাক্ষর. Should drivers see one word everywhere? If so, which?

4. **The line:** "Pre-trip walkaround"
   The walk round the van to check it is called চারপাশ পরীক্ষা in some lines and পরিদর্শন or "চারপাশ দেখা" in others. Which one word should drivers see everywhere?

## French (fr-safety.csv)

## Gujarati (gu-safety.csv)

1. **The line:** "Contacted the closure instead of following the diversion."
   The English says the van hit the closure (a contact). Does this say it hit it, or only that it reached it? If only reached, please correct.

2. **The line:** "A dent is visible here."
   Is this the natural word for a dent in a van's panel (a pushed-in metal surface)? The current word may read as a hole or pit in the ground.

3. **The line:** "You get as far as setting it down before the bright SIGNATURE REQUIRED sticker catches your eye. Left unattended, it is a failed delivery with your name on it. You pick it back up and ring the bell properly."
   The sticker text SIGNATURE REQUIRED is left in English here and in a few other lines, as it is printed on the parcel. Would a Gujarati-speaking driver expect it in Gujarati instead? If so, please give the wording.

4. **The line:** "Eyes up and early every time: the earlier you see a hazard developing, the more time you have to deal with it."
   "નજર ઊંચી અને વહેલી" follows the English closely ("eyes up and early"). Does it read naturally to a driver, or is there a more usual way to say "look up and ahead, early"?

## Tamil (ta-safety.csv)

1. **The line:** "A dent is visible here."
   Is this the natural word for a dent in a van's panel (a pushed-in metal surface)? The current word may read as a hole or pit in the ground.

2. **The line:** "Using the handheld while moving"
   Most lines say ஹேண்ட்ஹெல்ட், but this and 6 other lines say கைக்கருவி. Which would a Tamil-speaking driver understand at once?

3. **The line:** "Read the brief and every manifest page at Dispatch. Inspect the tires and lights, and request a repair when needed. Match each package to the manifest, scan it and load its numbered shelf. Report missing or unlisted packages, confirm the load count and secure the restraints."
   On its own the game spells it டிஸ்பாட்ச், but with an ending, as here, it writes டிஸ்பாச்சில், டிஸ்பாச்சர். Which spelling is right, and should they all match?

4. **The line:** "Photo ID and an adult signature are required. The recipient must be 21 or older."
   Most lines say கையொப்பம், but this one and about 11 others say கையெழுத்து. Should drivers see one word everywhere? If so, which?
